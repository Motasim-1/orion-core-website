About the engineer behind ORION.

Building intelligent systems across software, AI and electronics.

My work brings together software engineering, AI systems and electronics. I hold a bachelor's degree in Electronics, Informatics and Technology from the University of Oslo. I work with Python, C++, API development, ESP32, PCB design and technical analysis.

ORION is the largest AI software system in my portfolio. It is built around a single Core that coordinates specialized capabilities, maintains context and manages controlled execution.

I also work on embedded engineering concepts and a Python measurement interface for sensiBel microphone analysis. I am interested in technical collaboration on practical, ambitious projects.
